## What every account gets today (free)

Creating a free account at [calculatorlib.com](https://calculatorlib.com) already unlocks:

- **API tokens** (`clk_…`) at [/account/api-tokens](https://calculatorlib.com/account/api-tokens) — raises your MCP rate limit from 60 to 600 requests/minute. See [Authentication](/authentication).
- **MCP usage logs** at [/account/mcp-logs](https://calculatorlib.com/account/mcp-logs) — see every call made with your tokens.
- **Saved calculations** — star a calculator or save a result with its inputs to revisit later.

## How quotas work

Two independent layers:

1. **Per-minute rate limits** (live today, all tiers): 60 req/min anonymous, 600 req/min with a token — see [Rate limits](/rate-limits). These protect the service and don't change with your plan.
2. **Monthly API quotas** (per the table above): a commercial allowance counted across your MCP and API calls each calendar month. Exceeding it returns `429` with an `X-Quota-Remaining: 0` header until the month resets.

<Note>
During pre-launch, monthly quotas are **not yet enforced** — only the per-minute limits apply. Enforcement begins when paid plans go live, with the free allowance set high enough that typical personal use never hits it.
</Note>

## Billing details (paid tiers)

- **Hosted checkout** — payment runs entirely on the payment provider's hosted pages; your card details never touch CalculatorLib servers.
- **Self-serve portal** — upgrade, downgrade, change card, or cancel any time from [/account/billing](https://calculatorlib.com/account/billing).
- **Cancellation** — cancelling keeps your paid entitlements until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account drops to Free. Nothing is deleted.
- **Grace on payment failure** — a failed renewal doesn't cut access immediately; there's a short grace window while the payment retries.
- **Soft downgrade for embeds** — if a subscription lapses, embeds you've placed on other sites **keep working**; they revert to free-tier behavior (badge shown, domain lock released) rather than breaking.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for teams that need the platform under their own terms:

- **Invoice billing** (annual), no card required
- **Unlimited API volume** and embed domains
- **SLA-backed support** with a dedicated contact
- Custom terms (DPA, procurement paperwork) as needed

Enterprise accounts are provisioned manually — get in touch via [calculatorlib.com](https://calculatorlib.com) and we'll set your organization up directly.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I need a paid plan to use the API or MCP server?">
No. The REST API and MCP server are free — anonymous use included. Paid tiers raise the monthly volume ceiling and add platform features (badge-free embeds, analytics, ad-free browsing); they don't gate basic access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which plan do I need to embed a calculator?">
None — embedding is free for everyone. Paid tiers remove the "Powered by CalculatorLib" badge and add domain locking and analytics. See [Embed](/guides/embed).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What counts against the monthly API quota?">
Executed calls: MCP `tools/call` and REST `execute`/`convert` requests. Discovery calls (search, schemas, UI lookups) are effectively free — they're covered by the per-minute limits only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I try a paid tier before it launches?">
Join the waitlist at [calculatorlib.com/pricing](https://calculatorlib.com/pricing) — waitlist members get first access when checkout opens.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
