All three authenticate with your `clk_` [API token](https://calculatorlib.com/account/api-tokens) and share the two building blocks you'll recognize from [Zapier](/guides/zapier): a **Run Calculator** action (backed by `POST /api/calculator/execute/{slug}`) and, where the platform supports it, a **Calculation Completed** trigger — the `calculation.completed` [webhook](/guides/webhooks) from your keyed embeds.

## Make (Integromat)

App **modules** for running a calculator inside a Make scenario.

## Pipedream

Prebuilt **components**: a *Run Calculator* action and a *Calculation Completed* webhook **source** (it subscribes via `POST /api/webhooks` and unsubscribes via `DELETE /api/webhooks/{id}`).

## Power Automate

A **custom connector** (Swagger 2.0) you import into a flow to run calculators.

<Note>
The **Calculation Completed** trigger/source relies on webhooks — a **Builder plan and above** feature — and your embeds must carry your embed key. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks) and [Plans & pricing](/platform/plans).
</Note>
